# Hearthlands code map

One line per game file. Engine files are in Hydro's `docs/CodeMap.md`.

## Game/src

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `GameRegistration.*` | `RegisterGame()`: the compiled game's id (`hearthlands`), then the generated data and script reflection |
| `Generated/*` | Written by Hydro's reflection generator on every build; never edit |
| `Data/SeasonDefinition.h` | `SeasonDefinition` (`HY_DATA` table, `Assets/Data/Seasons.hytable`): a season's order and days; the snowline's move, `Flow`, `Melt`, `Growth`, `Life`; the animals: `Migrants`, `Burrowed`; the look: `GroundSnow`, `Ice`, `Fade`, `Tint`, `LeafTint`; farming (Nov 12): `Harvest` (wild plant food, blended), `Sowing`, `Frost` |
| `Data/AnimalDefinition.h` | `AnimalDefinition` (`HY_DATA` table, `Assets/Data/Animals.hytable`): a species: `AnimalKind` (Walker, Bird: sprite sheets; Fish, Insect, Flock: particles), its sheet, frames and rates, speeds, flee distance, particle look; where it lives (tiles, moisture, height, cover, water; `Habitat` (`WaterHabitat`) and `MinHabitat`), `Hidden` / `DiscoveryText` (a discovery), a fish's `SpriteIndex` and `LeapsPerMinute`, `Density`, births, spreading, newcomers, prey, `Migrates` / `Hibernates`, groups and how many are shown |
| `Data/PeopleDefinition.h` | `PeopleDefinition` (`HY_DATA` table, `Assets/Data/Peoples.hytable`): a people: names for its settlements and villagers, its sheet (idle, walk, work x 4 facings), frames and walk speed; its centre (`CentreObjectId`, `CentreName`, `GlowColor`, `Smoke`, `CentreMinLevel`: a mine up in the hills); what land it needs within `Radius` (fresh water, food, wood; `NeedObjectIds` / `NeedObjects`: old trees for elves; `NeedTileIds` / `NeedTiles`: mountain rock for dwarves) and likes; settling (`ArrivePerDay` for the first group, `SettleDecay`, `SettleSpacing`, group size, branching: `BranchFromLevel`, `BranchPerDay`, `BranchReach`); food eaten, gathered per gatherer, hunted per hunter (`HuntAnimalIds`), felling per woodcutter, foresters (`PlantObjectIds`, `PlantPerDay`, `ForesterShare`), births and leaving; branches (`EvolvesFromId`, `EvolvePerDay`: wood elves from elves); `Levels` (`PeopleLevel`: name; people, shelters, food days, old trees, rock to reach it; its shelter, people per shelter, wood, most people, store days, a line); feuds (`Dislikes`, `DislikeRadius`, `TensionPerDay`, `GuardShare`); farming (Nov 12): `FarmTileId` (the farmland they work, -1: none), `FarmFromLevel`, `CropObjectIds` (sown, growing, ripe), `CropDays`, `CropFood`, `SowPerDay`, `HarvestPerDay`, `SoilWear`; `FieldsWorked` (fields the farmers work without a barn) |
| `Data/BuildingDefinition.h` | `BuildingDefinition` (`HY_DATA` table, `Assets/Data/Buildings.hytable`; Nov 12) + `BuildingKind` (Store, HuntingLodge, Barn): a people's building besides its shelters: `BuildingId`, `PeopleId`, the object put up, the talent that makes it known, `MinLevel`, `Wood`, where it stands (`RingFrom` / `RingTo`), what it lifts (`StoreDays`, `HuntKeep`, `Fields`), `Description` |
| `Data/TalentDefinition.h` | `TalentDefinition` (`HY_DATA` table, `Assets/Data/Talents.hytable`): a talent's id, `TalentBranch` (Water, Land, Life, Insight, Peoples) and row, cost, required talent, `TalentEffect` (UnlockTile, MoistureReach, SpringHead, SpringFrom, ClueClarity, ClueFrom, BrushSize, GrowthSpeed, LifeSpeed, AnimalRoom, AnimalSight, SoilRichness, ForestCreep: forests creep onto bare ground, `GroundwaterView` (the G overlay), `OldGrowth` (ancient trees), `Settlers` (`TargetId`'s people settle on their own: the best owned chance a day counts), `FeudCalm` (less tension between peoples), `Evolve` (`TargetId`'s branch may arise from its kin)) and amount, a description line; `RequiresMilestoneId` (a milestone reached first, -1: none); `TalentEffect::Building` (TargetId's people may build the building with BuildingId = Amount) |
| `Data/MilestoneDefinition.h` | `MilestoneDefinition` (`HY_DATA` table, `Assets/Data/Milestones.hytable`): a first-time event giving talent points: `MilestoneKind` (Tiles of a type, Objects of a kind, Springs, Level, Animals of a species or all, Species living (of an `AnimalKind` or all), RichSoil, Settlers, Habitat: water tiles of a `WaterHabitat`), its target and count; `Settlers` per people (`TargetId` = PeopleId, -1 all), `Ancient` (ancient trees); `Hidden` (a ? card until reached) with its `Hint`, `MilestoneTrack`; `SettlementLevel` (a settlement of a people reaching a level: `TargetId`) |
| `Data/TerrainTypeDefinition.h` | `TerrainTypeDefinition` (`HY_DATA` table, `Assets/Data/TerrainTypes.hytable`): a terrain type's tile id, `TerrainRole` (Land, Water, Riverbed, Rock, Snow), brush name, colour, priority (0 to 15), unlocks (from the start, or after `UnlockCount` tiles of `UnlockTileId`), `Shelter` (moisture a tile walled in by it keeps), its soil (`Fertility`, `Sand`, `Living`), `Roots` (share of the groundwater its plants reach: painted grass holds over it), `FromLevel` / `BelowTileId` (rock and snow from a height, and what they turn back into), `CliffColor` (its walls) and generation ranges |
| `Data/ObjectDefinition.h` | `ObjectDefinition` (`HY_DATA` table, `Assets/Data/Objects.hytable`): bushes, trees, reeds, rocks per growth stage: sprite, `Variants` (a tile picks one) and size, `ObjectKind` (Building: a settlers' fire or shelter, never sprouting), `Food` and `Wood` for settlers, `Roots` (groundwater reached), `Canopy` (shade given), `ShadeEffect` (sprouting under trees or in the open), where it lives (tile, moisture, neighbours, `MinLevel` / `MaxLevel`), sprouting and spreading (`ChancePerLevel`: likelier higher up; `SoilEffect`: on rich soil), spacing, growing into the next stage, start density; `Ancient` (an old-woods stage, reached only with Old woods under a closed canopy) |
| `Data/TerrainRuleDefinition.h` | `TerrainRuleDefinition` (`HY_DATA` table, `Assets/Data/TerrainRules.hytable`): land changing by itself: from, to, moisture range, neighbours, chance, `SoilEffect` (likelier on rich soil, or on poor) |
| `State/TileMap.h` | `TileMap`: the world's tile ids, `Get` / `Set` / `InBounds`, a revision that moves on every change, `Replace`, counts per id (`GetCount`), a change log (`TakeChanges`); `FromTiles` (a map from saved tiles) |
| `State/SeasonState.h` | `SeasonState`: the season now (name, index, next, blend, day, year) and its blended values (`SeasonRules::At`) |
| `State/TalentState.h` | `TalentState`: points to spend and earned, owned talents, reached milestones, springs found, `Bonus` per effect (what the owned talents add up to), `Settling` per PeopleId (`SettleChance`: the best owned Settlers amount), `Evolving` per PeopleId (`MayEvolve`) |
| `State/SoilState.h` | `SoilState`: per tile the soil's richness (`Fertility`) and sand share, 0 to 255, and a revision |
| `State/SettlementState.h` | `SettlementState`: the settlements (`Settlement`: id, name, centre tile, its people, `Level`, people, food and wood stored, felling and building owed, shelters and `Room`, `OldShelters` (from below its level), what the land offers (water, food, wood, `Needed` old trees, `NeededTiles` rock), `Villagers` (`Villager`: id, name, `VillagerJob` (Resting, Gatherer, Hunter, Woodcutter, Builder, Guard, Hurt, Forester), days hurt and lived), `SettlementFocus` (None, Food, Wood, Building: the player's), `Tension` and `FeudWith` (the nearest disliked settlement), `Planting` owed, `EvolveTo` / `EvolveHave` / `EvolveWant` / `EvolveAllowed` (the branch it could become)), the next id, `SearchWait` (per people: days before looking for a place again), `Founded` (per people: names used), `Ruins` (where settlements emptied: new groups keep away a while), a revision; the `Farmer` job; `FarmWork`, `Fields`, `FieldsGrowing`, `FieldsRipe`, `Harvests`; `Buildings` (BuildingIds standing, found on the last update), `Planned` (the next to build), `FieldsMost` (fields worked) |
| `State/WildlifeState.h` | `WildlifeState`: the map in square regions (`RegionSize` 16); per species (the Animals table's rows) and region the animals living there (`Population`) and what the land holds (`Capacity`); where the next update goes on; `RegionOf`, `Total` |
| `State/HeightMap.h` | `HeightMap`: the land's level per tile (0 the ground, 0 off the map), `Get` / `Set`, a revision, `Replace`, a change log (`TakeChanges`); `FromLevels` (from saved levels) |
| `State/Hydrology.h` | `Hydrology`: per tile water head, source flag, `Carried` moisture (spread from fresh water), `Rain` (from the height), `Melt` (meltwater running there, 255 at the snow), `Groundwater` and `Moisture` (what the land has: carried or rain, more where wet grass walls a tile in), the active tiles for the next tick; `Sealed` (dry creek beds that never spring by themselves) |
| `State/ObjectLayer.h` | `ObjectLayer`: the object id on each tile (0 none), a revision, a count |
| `State/Random.h` | `Random`: the game's seeded generator (xorshift64*), passed into rules; `GetState` / `SetState` (saved) |
| `State/SaveBytes.h` | `SaveWriter` / `SaveReader`: a save file's bytes (values, length-first strings and vectors) in sections with a tag, a length and a version (`SaveTag`); the reader never reads past its end (`IsGood`) |
| `State/HearthlandsSession.h` | `HearthlandsSession`: the run's state (the tile map, its heights and `LevelHeight`, the groundwater and how plainly it shows (`ClueClarity`, `ClueFrom`), the unlocked brush tile ids, the land mask: paintable inside the coast, the brush (`BrushSettings`) and where it points and paints (for the animals), the wildlife, the settlements, the selected settlement (its detail open), renaming and camera focus the HUD asks for, notices for the HUD, the clock, whether the pointer is over the HUD, whether the meltwater needs working out again), registered in `GameRegistration.cpp`; `FieldTileId` and `FarmReach` (where farmland may be painted); `CameraTile` / `CameraZoom` (where the camera looks, saved), `Loading` (a save was loaded: scripts wait for the scene to start again) |
| `State/SimulationClock.h` | `SimulationClock`: speed (0 = paused, 1, 3, 10), world seconds, ticks run; `GetDay` (60 world seconds a day) |
| `Rules/TerrainGenRules.h` | `TerrainGenRules`: a tile map from a seed (layered value noise for height and moisture, type ranges from data), `Levels` (heights from the same noise); `ShapeCoast` rounds the map off with a wavy sea rim, `AddBeach` lines it with sand, `LandOf` (inside the coast; inland lakes count as land), `StepsToSea` |
| `Rules/BrushRules.h` | `BrushRules`: the brush's maths: `RayToGround`, `MapPoint` / `OnMap` (map-centred point to map tiles), `Stroke` (joins a drag), `Stamp` (round brush around a point, never on the sea), `Radii` / `StepRadius` (sizes), `BrushTile`, `StartingBrushes` |
| `Rules/WaterRules.h` | `WaterRules`: water heads (strongest neighbour, or `MeltHead` next to a `Melt` tile, less a loss, smaller in wide channels), riverbed filling and drying (dug riverbed over groundwater springs; `FillHead`: more by a wide channel's banks, so river ends are rounded), moisture from fresh water (fading in curves), snowmelt (`MeltMoisture`), groundwater, rain (`RainPerLevel`) and shelter (a tile with 5 of 8 wet sheltering neighbours keeps their mean carried moisture plus their `Shelter`, never passed on); `Start`, `Step` (one tile per tick), `FlowHead`, `TargetMoisture`, `ShelteredMoisture`, `UpdateRain`, `UpdateMelt`; a `Sealed` bed never springs, and filled in it loses the seal |
| `Rules/GroundwaterRules.h` | `GroundwaterRules`: the hidden water per tile (`Generate`, its own noise in patches), `Springs` / `SpringHead` (a dug riverbed over it becomes a source), `Wetness` (damp above it), `Clue` (how plainly the surface shows it: clarity and threshold, which talents will move) |
| `Rules/SeasonRules.h` | `SeasonRules`: `Sorted`, `At` (the season `days` into the run, blending into the next over its last days), `Step` (rounding, so the simulation and the materials change in steps) |
| `Rules/SoilRules.h` | `SoilRules`: the soil: `FromTypes`, `Start`, `Texture` / `Quality` (richness times texture, best at loam), `Factor` (a chance on this soil; plain dirt is `Neutral`), `Target` (type, humus, silt, talents), `Paint` (a painted type mixes in: dirt over sand makes loam), `Step` (drifting towards the target on random samples), `Qualities` (the soil view, rich tiles); `Lookup` for the growth rules |
| `Rules/SettlementRules.h` | `SettlementRules`: a settlement's life: `Found` (a people's centre on free, dry land, high enough for them; its villagers, or a branch's movers), `Rename`, `NewVillager` / `SyncVillagers` (names in turn, II for a second round), `Look` (fresh water, plant food, game, wood, their shelters and the room they give, old trees and rock within the radius), `Scores` / `Meets` / `Liking` / `NeededShare`, `LevelOf` / `RoomFor` / `ShelterHolds`, `AssignJobs` (food to eat by the better of gathering and hunting, guards in tension, a woodcutter and a builder while it needs shelters, foresters (`Plants`), food to store, then the focus; jobs kept while wanted), `NextNeeds` / `NeedsMet` (what the next level needs, have and want), `Update` (healing, jobs, gathering and hunting the wildlife, eating, growing, felling, building the level's shelter, levelling up, leaving; gone with its centre, emptied: its centre taken down), `Lose` (a villager killed), `Hunt`, `Fell`, `ShelterLevel` / `Older` / `CountOld` / `Upgrade` (on a new level one older shelter is rebuilt as the level's at once, then the builders rebuild the rest with spare wood), `Plant` (a sapling that `ObjectRules::Fits` beyond the shelters), `Build`, `People` (all settlers, for the milestones), `JobName`; `World` has `Heights`; `World` has `Soil`, `SoilSettings`, `FieldTileId` (never built on) and the season's `Harvest`, `Growth`, `Sowing`, `Frost`; `Farming`; farmers by the ripe fields to harvest and, while sowing, the empty ones to sow; buildings (Nov 12): `World` has `Buildings` and `Talents`; `Look` finds their buildings; `Update` plans (`Plan`), counts the store with a food store's `StoreDays`, the meat with a lodge's `HuntKeep`, puts up the planned building (`PutUp`, in its ring) after new shelters and before older ones are rebuilt; builders and woodcutters for it (`Report::BuiltBuilding`) |
| `Rules/FarmRules.h` | `FarmRules`: farming on painted farmland (Nov 12): `Farms` (a people with `FarmTileId` from `FarmFromLevel`), `Farmland` (its tiles within `Radius`, nearest first), `Count` (`Fields`), `Fertility` (soil quality) and `Wetness` (moisture), `Grow` (crops stage by stage, faster on rich, moist soil and in a growing season; frost withers them), `Tend` (farmers harvest the ripe, food by fertility, wearing the soil; then sow the empty while sowing), `Step` (both for a settlement, run before `SettlementRules::Update`), `FarmersWanted`; `Reach` / `MayPaint` (the Farmland brush paints only within Radius of a settlement that farms); farmers sow no more than `FieldsMost` fields (their people's `FieldsWorked` and a barn's `Fields`) |
| `Rules/BuildingRules.h` | `BuildingRules` (Nov 12): a settlement's buildings besides its shelters (pure): `RowOf` / `Find`, `Known` (its talent owned), `Allowed` (their people, level, known), `Stands`, `Of` (what the standing ones add up to: `Bonus`), `Wanted` (the next: allowed, not standing, needed by `Needs`: the store nearly full, hunting, farmland beyond what is worked), `Lines` (the panel: built, building, when needed, from level n, not known yet), `Lower` |
