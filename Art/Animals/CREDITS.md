# Animal art: where it comes from

`scripts/art/make_animal_sheets.py` makes the game's sheets in `Project/Assets/Sprites/Animals/` from these packs. Keep this
list in the game's credits.

| Pack | In `Source/` | Used for | Licence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| "32 x 32 Pixel Isometric Tiles" critters by scrabling, https://scrabling.itch.io/pixel-isometric-tiles | `Critters/` (with the Aseprite files) | Deer (the stag), Boar, Wolf, Badger | CC BY 4.0; the page also says no attribution is required, but credit scrabling anyway. Not to be shared on its own, only inside the game |
| "[LPC] Birds" by bluecarrot16, https://opengameart.org/node/90156 (as far as we know: 15 birds, fly and walk in 4 directions) | `Birds/` | Sparrow, Robin, Cardinal, Bluejay, Eagle, Egret, Crow, Pigeon | CC-BY 4.0 / 3.0, CC-BY-SA, GPL, OGA-BY 3.0 (pick one): credit bluecarrot16 and the artists the page lists |
| "Animated Wild Animals" by ScratchIO, https://opengameart.org/content/animated-wild-animals | `SideView/` | Not in the game (side view, twice the size): references for PixelLab (fox, rabbit, bear) | CC0 |

Before a release, check each page again for its exact credit line.
